# Module 1: Cybersecurity Basics

**Audience:** All users  
**Duration:** 5–7 minutes  
**Format:** Video + quiz

## Covers

- What cybersecurity means
- Why employees are targeted
- Common workplace cyber risks
- Reporting suspicious activity
- Secure habits

## Scenario

You receive an unexpected message asking you to open a document urgently. The sender looks familiar, but the email address is unusual.

## Quiz

1. Cybersecurity is only IT's responsibility. True or False?  
   **Answer:** False

2. What should you do with a suspicious email?  
   **Answer:** Report it using the approved method.

3. Why do attackers use urgency?  
   **Answer:** To make users act before thinking.

# Module 2: Phishing Awareness

**Audience:** All users  
**Duration:** 7 minutes  
**Format:** Interactive module + Red Flag Lens

## Covers

- Fake sender names
- Lookalike domains
- Suspicious links
- Attachments
- Urgency pressure
- Reporting phishing

## Scenario

An email says your mailbox will be blocked in 15 minutes unless you verify your account.

## Quiz

1. Should you enter your password into an unexpected login page?  
   **Answer:** No.

2. What is a lookalike domain?  
   **Answer:** A domain designed to look like a real one.

3. What is the safest action?  
   **Answer:** Report the email.
