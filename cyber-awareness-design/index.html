Click a red flag

Phishing is a trust trick.

Attackers copy normal workplace messages, add urgency, and push users toward unsafe links or attachments. This lab teaches users to pause, inspect and report.

1Check sender domain, not only display name.
2Urgency is often used to stop careful thinking.
3Links and attachments need verification before action.
Safe simulation rule: never collect real passwords. Clicking should open a training page with red flags and microlearning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can customers upload their own content?

Yes. Customer admins can upload videos, PDFs, policies, quizzes, puzzles and custom phishing templates with draft, preview and publish workflow.

Is the free trial extendable?

Yes. The central super admin can extend the 30-day trial based on customer requirement.

Do phishing simulations collect real passwords?

No. Simulations should be safe by design. Clicks should redirect to training pages and never collect real credentials.

Can SMTP be configured customer-wise?

Yes. Customers can configure SMTP host, port, sender, encryption, reply-to, test email and notification templates.

Does it support compliance topics?

Yes. The content roadmap includes DPDPA, RBI, SEBI, CERT-In, NIST, CIS, ISO 27001, ITIL, MITRE and red/blue team concepts.
