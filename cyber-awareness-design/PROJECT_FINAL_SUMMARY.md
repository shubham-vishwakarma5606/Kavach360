## Key Product Positioning

Kavach360 is a multi-tenant cyber awareness, phishing simulation and compliance training SaaS for businesses. It includes employee training, phishing simulations, DPDPA, RBI, SEBI, NIST, MITRE, IT support frameworks, content upload, SMTP, certificates, reports, scam contribution and customer-controlled branding.
