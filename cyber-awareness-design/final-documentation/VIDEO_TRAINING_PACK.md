# Kavach360 Video Training Pack

This pack contains recommended video training modules for the Kavach360 platform.

Each video should be between **5 and 10 minutes**.

---

# Video 1: Welcome to Kavach360

**Duration:** 5 minutes  
**Audience:** All users  
**Mode:** Beginner

## Objective

Introduce the platform and explain why cybersecurity awareness matters.

## Script Outline

1. Welcome message
2. What Kavach360 is
3. Why employees are targeted
4. What users will learn
5. How to complete training
6. How to report suspicious activity

## Narration Script

Welcome to Kavach360, your cybersecurity awareness and phishing defence platform.

This training is not about making you a technical expert. It is about helping you recognise common cyber risks in everyday work, such as suspicious emails, unsafe links, fake login pages, mobile scams, data handling mistakes and phishing simulations.

Inside the portal, you will see your assigned modules, quizzes, certificates and progress. If your organisation runs phishing simulations, remember that the goal is learning, not blame.

The most important habits are simple: pause before you click, verify unusual requests and report anything suspicious quickly.

# Video 2: How Phishing Tricks Good Employees

**Duration:** 7 minutes  
**Audience:** All users  
**Mode:** Beginner

## Objective

Explain phishing in simple workplace language.

## Covers

- What phishing is
- Fake sender names
- Urgency pressure
- Suspicious links
- Attachments
- Reporting

## Narration Script

Phishing is a trick where an attacker pretends to be someone you trust. It may look like an email from IT, HR, a vendor, a courier company or even a senior manager.

A phishing message usually tries to make you act quickly. It may say your password will expire, your salary document is ready, your package is blocked or a payment is urgent.

Before clicking, check the sender address, the link, the attachment type and the language used. If something feels unusual, do not investigate alone. Report it using the approved reporting method.

# Video 3: Red Flag Lens — Spot the Suspicious Email

**Duration:** 6 minutes  
**Audience:** All users  
**Mode:** Beginner / Intermediate

## Objective

Teach users to identify phishing warning signs.

## Covers

- Sender domain
- Urgency
- Login links
- Attachments
- Grammar and formatting

## Interactive Moment

Show a suspicious email and ask users to click the red flags.

## Narration Script

Let us inspect this email together. The display name says IT Support, but the actual domain is not the official company domain. That is the first red flag.

The subject says the mailbox will be blocked in 15 minutes. That is urgency pressure. Attackers use urgency to stop people from thinking carefully.

The link points to an unfamiliar website. Always use official portals or bookmarks instead of clicking unexpected login links.

Finally, the attachment is an executable file. Unexpected executable files should not be opened.

# Video 4: Password, OTP and MFA Safety

**Duration:** 6 minutes  
**Audience:** All users  
**Mode:** Beginner

## Covers

- Strong passwords
- Password reuse
- OTP scams
- MFA approval fraud
- MFA fatigue

## Narration Script

Your password protects access to company systems, email, files and customer data. Never reuse work passwords on personal websites.

OTP codes and MFA approvals are also sensitive. No genuine IT, bank or support person should ask you to share an OTP. If you receive repeated MFA prompts that you did not initiate, deny the request and report it immediately.

# Video 5: DPDPA and Personal Data Protection

**Duration:** 8–10 minutes  
**Audience:** All employees  
**Mode:** Beginner / Intermediate

## Covers

- What personal data is
- Customer data
- Employee data
- Data sharing
- Breach reporting
- No-delay reporting

## Narration Script

Personal data is any data that can identify a person directly or indirectly. Names, phone numbers, email addresses, employee IDs, customer notes, photos, location data and even some metadata can be personal data.

Under DPDPA, employees must handle personal data carefully. Use approved systems, share only what is necessary and report mistakes immediately.

If personal data is sent to the wrong person, do not delete evidence or wait to see what happens. Report it immediately to the correct internal team.

# Video 6: Mobile, WhatsApp and SMS Scams

**Duration:** 7 minutes  
**Audience:** All users  
**Mode:** Beginner

## Covers

- Smishing
- WhatsApp impersonation
- OTP fraud
- UPI fraud
- Courier scams
- Fake manager messages

## Narration Script

Mobile scams are common because people respond quickly on phones. A fake SMS may say your courier is blocked, your electricity will be disconnected or your KYC is pending.

WhatsApp scams may use a manager’s photo and ask for urgent payment, gift cards or confidential data.

Do not trust urgency or profile photos. Verify using official channels before acting.

# Video 7: Fake Apps and Play Store Safety

**Duration:** 6 minutes  
**Audience:** All users  
**Mode:** Beginner

## Covers

- Fake apps
- Cloned apps
- App permissions
- APK risks
- Fake loan/investment apps

## Narration Script

Fake apps often copy the name, logo or colour of trusted brands. Before installing an app, check the official developer name, reviews, download count and permissions.

Be careful if a simple app asks for SMS, contacts, accessibility, microphone or camera access without a clear reason.

Avoid installing APK files from unknown websites.

# Video 8: IoT and Smart Office Security

**Duration:** 7 minutes  
**Audience:** Intermediate users / IT / Admin teams

## Covers

- Routers
- CCTV
- Smart TVs
- Printers
- Default passwords
- Firmware updates
- Network separation

## Narration Script

IoT devices are often forgotten after installation. Cameras, printers, routers, biometric devices and smart TVs may store or transmit sensitive information.

Change default passwords, update firmware and keep IoT devices on a separate network where possible. If your role includes office devices or facility systems, treat them as part of the security environment.

# Video 10: NIST, MITRE, Red Team and Blue Team Concepts

**Duration:** 8–10 minutes  
**Audience:** Intermediate / Expert

## Covers

- NIST CSF
- MITRE ATT&CK
- Red team
- Blue team
- Purple team
- SOC
- MTTD / MTTR

## Narration Script

NIST Cybersecurity Framework can be understood as five simple actions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond and Recover.

MITRE ATT&CK is a map of attacker behaviour used by defenders to understand common tactics and improve detection.

Red teams test defences in an authorised way. Blue teams monitor and respond. Purple teams combine both to improve security.

MTTD means how long it takes to detect an issue. MTTR means how long it takes to respond or recover. Fast reporting by employees improves both.

# Video 11: Safe Phishing Simulation for Admins

**Duration:** 8 minutes  
**Audience:** Customer admins / campaign managers

## Covers

- Campaign setup
- Consent checklist
- Approved domains
- User assignment
- No credential capture
- Training landing page
- Reports

## Narration Script

A phishing simulation must be safe and approved. Before launching, confirm management approval, HR or legal review, approved domain use and training landing page readiness.

Never collect real passwords. The goal is to measure awareness and provide immediate learning.

After the campaign, review click rate, report rate, department risk and users who need retraining.

# Suggested Quiz After Each Video

Each video should end with:

1. One knowledge question
2. One scenario question
3. One action question

Example:

**Question:** You receive an urgent password expiry email with an unfamiliar link. What should you do?

Correct answer: Report it using the approved reporting method and do not enter credentials.
